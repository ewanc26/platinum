# Images on the Mac side

Date: 2026-10-31.

## Sources

- https://github.com/bluesky-social/atproto/issues/3922 (feed_thumbnail /
  feed_fullsize presets)
- https://bsky.app/profile/bsky.app/post/3k... (2026 post announcing 2 MB /
  4000x4000 upload limits; search result, not fetched)
- https://github.com/bluesky-social/atproto/tree/main/packages/bsky/src/image
  (image service; raw fetch timed out)
- Local `macos9/image_blob.c` (PLIM reader) and `docs/IMAGES.md` (bridge format)

## Findings

- Post images are served as JPEG by default; PNG can be requested for some
  assets. Thumbnails are addressed by the CDN URL's `feed_thumbnail` preset and
  full-size by `feed_fullsize`; avatars are a separate small preset.
- The bridge's PLIM format was a way to avoid decoding on the Mac. With the
  bridge gone, the Mac needs a JPEG (and possibly PNG) decoder, or the feature
  must remain unavailable.
- A 4000x4000 RGBA image is 64 MB, far beyond a small OS 9 partition. Even a
  1000x1000 RGB decode is 3 MB plus the decoder's tables. Any native path must
  fetch a thumbnail-sized asset and enforce a pixel and byte cap before
  allocating.
- No C89 decoder has been selected. Candidates need a licence and memory audit;
  a decoder that assumes `size_t`-heavy allocation or C99 is not suitable.
  Wolfram has image code, but its Mac OS 9 transport target deliberately excludes
  the C23 image module.
- The existing `image_blob.c` only parses Platinum's PLIM; it cannot decode JPEG
  or PNG.

## Decision

- Do not remove the bridge image route until a decoder is chosen and tested.
  Record this as a blocker rather than shipping a broken image path.
- If a decoder is adopted later, fetch `feed_thumbnail` at a bounded size,
  reject anything above the byte/pixel cap before decoding, and draw through the
  existing QuickDraw path. No 4000x4000 full-size fetch on a 1990s Mac.
- A placeholder (initials or a generic icon) is acceptable temporarily, but it
  must not be presented as image support.

## Not verified

- The exact CDN format and preset names at the current API version. The issue
  and the public post are secondary evidence; the image service source was not
  fetched.
- Decoder licences and 68k compatibility. None chosen.
